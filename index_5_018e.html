HOW TO PLAY

Build your mech. In the Hangar, equip parts (head, core, two arms, legs). Better parts raise Power. Spend skill points in Skills.

Battles. In Tactical mode you pick each move: tap a weapon, switch arms for a +10% combo, Brace when an enemy charges a HEAVY attack, and fire Overdrive when the meter fills. Prefer to watch? Switch to Auto any time. Speed and Skip are always there. Fights you play yourself in Tactical earn a hands-on bonus of +10% to +30% Fed-$ and XP, graded by combos, braced HEAVY hits and Overdrives.

Armor. Every enemy card shows its armor. Plated (▣) shrugs off guns (-30%) but cracks under melee (+30%). Shielded (◈) takes +30% from energy weapons and -20% from melee. Light (◇) takes +30% from guns. Your arm buttons turn green when they hit a weak spot and red when they're resisted. Switching arms between attacks adds a +10% combo.

HEAVY attacks. Every 3 or 4 turns an enemy charges a HEAVY that hits for 2.5x. You always get one turn of warning (HEAVY NEXT). Brace and you take 60% less. Bosses also have one SPECIAL attack they charge when badly hurt. In a boss fight, only a HEAVY or SPECIAL you didn't brace for can finish you off.

Field repair. After every fight you win on a mission, your crew repairs half the hull you're missing. Damage still adds up, so pick your fights.

Sets. Some head, core and legs parts belong to a named set (Warlord, Seraph, Gunslinger, Sentinel). Equip all 3 for a bonus. The Hangar shows your set progress.

Missions. Each contract costs fuel (Short 1, Medium 2, Long 3). You get 12 fuel a day. Roll the die to move along the path and beat the boss at the gate. Enemies scale to your mech's power, so gear upgrades won't make Hard easy.

Run away. During a mission fight, tap Run away to escape with the hull you have left. You can run twice; the second time ends the mission and you keep your loot. Running from a mini-boss or boss pushes you back so you face it again.

Decision points. Medium missions have one gold decision point a third of the way in, and Long missions add a second just past the mini-boss. You always stop there and pick between a steady road and a gamble. The gamble rides on a scout report: if it is right, the road is clear sailing; if it is wrong, there are enemies on almost every square (and every kill pays 50% extra).

Thrusters and tile info. Every mission gives you 2 thruster charges (3 on Long). Tap +1, +2 or +3 instead of Roll to move exactly that far: land on the crate, skip the trap. Tap any tile on the board to see what it does and the odds.

Tiles. White = Crossroads (pick a risky or a safe route), red = fight, gold = Fed-$, green = repair, yellow = power boost, pink = choose attack or armor, purple = crate, orange = trap, blue = mystery signal. Medium and Long missions have a mini-boss halfway, with a drop and a full repair right after.

If your mech is destroyed. You lose half the Fed-$ and every part you found that mission, even the mini-boss drop. On Easy contracts your fuel is refunded. Know when to run.

Arena. 5 tickets a day, 1 per battle. Beat the mechs above you to climb the ladder and earn rank.

Store. Spend credits on part packs, paints, fuel and tickets.
