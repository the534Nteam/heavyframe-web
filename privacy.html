HEAVYFRAME Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE]. Applies to the HEAVYFRAME: Mech Battles app and website.

HEAVYFRAME is published by Pixel & Palate Partners LLC, a Hawaii limited liability company ("we," "us"). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Game progress. Your pilot name, mechs, parts, credits, and settings are saved on your device. We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number, or address to play.

Purchases. When you buy something, Google Play processes the payment. We never see or store your card details. Our purchase service provider, RevenueCat, receives a random app user ID and a record of what you bought so your purchases can be confirmed and restored.

Ads. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, and how you interact with ads, to show ads and measure them. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings under Google, then Ads.

Support messages. If you send feedback or a support request, we keep the message and your pilot name so we can reply.

Diagnostics. Google Play and our service providers may collect basic crash and performance data about the app.

How we use it

To run the game, save your progress, process and restore purchases, show and measure ads, answer support requests, prevent cheating and fraud, and improve the game. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that run the game: Google (Play, Play Billing, AdMob) and RevenueCat. Each handles data under its own privacy policy. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Children

HEAVYFRAME is intended for players 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Progress on your device stays until you use Reset progress in the game or uninstall the app. To delete support messages or purchase records we hold, email us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including California and the European Union, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to certain uses. Email us to make a request.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, and payments are handled entirely by Google Play. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, notify players in the game.
